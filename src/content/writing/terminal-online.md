This site takes its appearance from the terminals in Fallout 3.

Astro builds the pages from Markdown. Solid 2 handles menu selection, sound
settings and the guitar interaction.

The Music page has a drawing of a Gibson Les Paul Special. Plugging it in plays
a synthesized E major chord.
