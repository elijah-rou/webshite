This terminal is just getting started.

It’s a place for writing about code, keeping a record of projects, and making room
for music. The screen takes its cues from Fallout 3: green text, a curved CRT,
and a small sound as you move around.

## On the other end of the cable

In the Music section, there’s a Gibson Les Paul Special. Plug it in to try a
synthesized E chord. Actual recordings will have their own space here when they’re ready.
