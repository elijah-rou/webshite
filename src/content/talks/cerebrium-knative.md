A Maintainer Track session, given with Dave Protasowski, a Knative maintainer.

It covers how Cerebrium, a serverless GPU platform serving customers around the
world at low latency and cost, runs on Knative: the operational problems met
across different environments, how Cerebrium solved them, and what was learned
about running Knative in production for AI workloads.
